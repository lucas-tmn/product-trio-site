Quick rules

Look

  • Paper background, white cards, navy for emphasis. Max 1 navy band per 2 sections.
  • Amber is a seasoning. Small amber text uses the darker accent-text.
  • Headings: Plus Jakarta Sans. Everything else: Inter. No third font.
  • Cards: 1px line, 14px corners, no shadows.
  • Photos: black-and-white portraits only. Deliverables are blurred and tagged "Anonymized".

Layout

  • 1120px wide, 24px gutters, 88px between sections (64px mobile).
  • Three columns become one on phones.
  • Articles: 720px wide text.

Words

  • Start with the result. Every claim has a number, a role or a decision.
  • Cut "we". Name the role instead.
  • No buzzwords. Sentence case everywhere.
  • Client names: anonymize ("Top-3 logistics provider in Vietnam").
  • [Brackets] = not final. Remove before launch.
  • Write English first; Vietnamese is a faithful translation, up to 25% longer.

Always

  • One primary button per screen.
  • Tap targets 44px or larger.
  • Visible focus ring. Never use color alone.
  • Form fields have a label above and a text error.
  • Motion is 150–250ms and optional.
